## Unresolved / caveats

1. **Beat ambiguity:** The "left, right, left, right" sequence (is3-footwork-2) and subsequent swapping steps (is3-footwork-5, is3-footwork-6) are clearly part of a counted pattern, but the teachers never explicitly say which beats these fall on. The cues preserve this ambiguity with `beat: —`.

2. **Whisper error:** "malebo" at 40.64s is a transcription error for "Malibu". The normalized transcript did not catch this because both are valid English-adjacent words. Corrected in cue text; error preserved in verbatim.

3. **Gender-specific styling:** Several arms cues mention differences between "guys" and "girls" (arm extension size, finger styling, shoulder rolling emphasis). These are presented as a single `both` cue with the verbatim preserving the distinction, because the *movement* is the same even if the *styling amplitude* differs.

4. **Count-along excluded:** Large sections of the transcript (e.g., 112.4s–190.82s, 269.68s–292.88s) consist of vocalised counting over demonstrations ("one, two, three, and five, six, malebo"). Per the brief, these are rhythm vocalisations, not drillable instructions, and are not parsed into cues.
