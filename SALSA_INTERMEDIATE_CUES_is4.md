## Notes on role

This is a solo steps class, so all cues are `role: 'both'`. There is no leader/follower distinction in solo footwork.

## Notes on beats

Several cues carry real beat numbers that the teachers explicitly called:
- **1, 2, 3**: basic steps forward
- **5, 6**: two jumps backwards  
- **7**: the freeze/pose

Where the teachers did not specify a beat (e.g., general concepts about head level, knee bending), `beat` is `—`.

## Notes on kind

Most cues are `footwork` (foot placement and movement) or `concept` (principles about how to execute the jump). Four cues are `rhythm` because they specify the timing pattern. Three cues are `arms` for the final abakwa pose.

The count from @56-75s is vocalised rhythm over demonstration ("one, two, hop, chick chick, kum") and is not parsed into separate cues per the brief's instruction that "the count-along is not instruction."
